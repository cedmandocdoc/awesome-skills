# Choosing Style

## Overview

The film's form: the 15 dials and how to combine them, the story styles, the hook types, the energy scale, the 13 presets with where each places the sold ≠ shown beats, and how to recommend 3 concepts. What the film says lives in [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md); how each dial's options look, sound and are voiced lives in [directing-picture.md](./directing-picture.md), [directing-sound.md](./directing-sound.md) and [directing-voice.md](./directing-voice.md).

## Guidelines

### The dials

A promotion is a stack of independent dials. Choose each on its own; no dial sets the others.

**Combining them:**

- **One signature device per film:** the one thing a viewer remembers (the slams, the exploded UI, the collage). Name it in [promotion-contract.md](./promotion-contract.md) → **promotion.md** → Spec.
- **Every other layer in one grammar:** a wild device on a plain world, or a wild world with plain cuts.
- Transitions vary in kind, never in style ([directing-picture.md](./directing-picture.md) → **Transitions**).
- The brand kit rides unchanged over every device ([directing-picture.md](./directing-picture.md) → **Brand kit**).
- The clock: [directing-sound.md](./directing-sound.md) → **Music is the clock**; the voice-led presets are named under **Presets**.

### Story styles

| Style | Beats | Awareness | Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kinetic Slam | Pain words slam on the beat over the old way → flash cut into the live app → payoff → callback → offer | Cold, problem-aware | A pain kept on paper (a time book, a ledger, a patient folder) |
| Problem → solution | Pain 0–2 s → the app by ~5 s → proof → CTA | Cold, problem-aware | A visible old way and one task; not when the old way is invisible |
| Result-first / reveal | Open on the payoff as the screen shows it ("12 payslips sent"), then rewind to how | Unaware, solution-aware | A result that reads at phone size |
| Before / after | Split: the old way vs the app, counted in steps, never clocks; no commentary | Problem- or solution-aware | Fewer steps; an after-state that reads in one frame. Risk: TikTok's ad policy restricts before-and-after comparisons and may cover a workflow split |
| Overheard chat | The story told through a flooding generic chat, then the app absorbs it | Problem-aware | Orders or requests that arrive by chat |
| Screen-recording style | The sample flow shot like a screen recording; on-screen text names each step; the sample label stays; never "real time" | Solution- or product-aware (retargeting) | Any app with one clear flow |
| Satisfying UI | Close-ups of UI snapping into place, clicks and ticks, no voice, a tidy end state | Cold scrollers | Grids, schedules, sorted lists |
| POV / day in the life | "POV: payday on site": phone in hand, a day compressed, anonymous hands only | Unaware; identity | Trades with a daily routine |
| Skit | A scripted mini-story with the app as the punchline: a labelled dramatization of the pain, or played by the seller's team | Unaware; shareable | Markets whose ads run on humour |
| Meme / trend hijack | Ride a live format; the music stays a Pixabay track in the trend's style | Unaware; needs speed | Short-video feeds. Risk: a trend dates within weeks |
| Listicle | "3 signs your payroll is costing you": numbered beats, each with proof | Weighing options | Any |
| Us vs the old way | A table or split against a generic alternative | Ready to switch | Any |
| Edutainment / Trojan horse | Teach a useful tip ("how to compute overtime"); the app appears in the last ~20 % | Ad-resistant viewers | A rule people compute by hand (pay, tax, dosage). Fact-check every figure against its official source |
| Founder / team-led | A real member of the seller's team to camera, lo-fi, one claim, one offer | Trust; cold or retargeting | A seller viewers must trust first; needs filming |
| Comment reply | Answers a real comment, then the demo | Engaged viewers | After posts draw real comments |
| Offer-first | The offer, with what it covers, from frame 1; never the bare word "free" | Most aware; retargeting | A strong offer |
| Lo-fi native | A notes-app or text-to-speech style post, phone-native type, the app proof at the end; no people | Cold; ad-resistant | Any |
| Seasonal deadline | A real dated obligation (a statutory pay date, a filing deadline) → the app handles it | Problem-aware, time-boxed | Apps tied to a calendar duty, in its season |

Blocked by the honesty rules ([promotion-contract.md](./promotion-contract.md) → **Honesty rules**):

- POV or day in the life where a stock, illustrated or AI person uses the app as a customer.
- A skit where a character uses or praises the app as a customer.
- "Watch me…" with an on-screen timer ("payroll for 12 crew in 30 s"); a rebuilt scene has no runtime, so no speed or time claim holds.
- A testimonial or case study until **Proof** holds a consenting customer's quote.

**Choose by awareness** (from the Study's audience):

| Audience is | Lead with |
| --- | --- |
| Problem-aware | Problem → solution, Kinetic Slam, before / after, overheard chat |
| Solution-aware | Result-first, screen-recording style, us vs the old way, listicle |
| Unaware or mass | POV, skit, satisfying UI, result-first, edutainment |
| Most aware (retargeting) | Offer-first, founder / team-led, comment reply |

Default: problem → solution or result-first for the master; before / after or result-first for the ≤ 15 s cut.

### Hook types

| Hook | Frame 0 | Example | Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pain shown | The old way mid-task | The notebook mid-calculation; a generic chat with 47 unread inside a drawn phone | Problem-aware |
| Kinetic call-out | The pain in 2–4 words, slammed on the beat | "PAYDAY. AGAIN. BY HAND?" | Cold, music-led |
| Directive / warning | A command in large type | "Stop computing payroll by hand." | Problem-aware |
| Call-out by trade | The trade's own label | "To every bakery owner…" | Niche trades |
| If-then | A situation the viewer can name | "If your order book is still a chat thread…" | A nameable situation |
| Number | Counts from the viewer's own work | "12 workers. 6 days. 1 photo." | Sceptical viewers |
| Contrast | Two states of one task (paper / app) | The paper sheet beside the filled screen | Ready to switch |
| Question / curiosity gap | The real input, with a question | "What if one photo finished payroll?" | Cold |
| Visual oddity / surprise | Something impossible in frame 1 | The notebook page morphs into a phone screen | Cold |
| Result shown | The finished result as the screen shows it | "12 payslips sent" | Unaware |
| Offer first | The offer with what it covers | "Your first month, free" | Warm audiences |
| Deadline | A real date | "Year-end payroll closes on <date>. Ready?" | Seasonal, a real due date |
| Person speaks first | A team member's face and first line | "I build apps for clinics. Here's one." | Founder / team-led |

The hook's timing, word count and first line follow [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Hook lines**.

### Energy scale

| Level | Feel | Cut rate (start here; tune by eye) | Type | Music | Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Calm-premium | Spacious, one message | 2–4 s holds | Refined, 400–600 | Ambient, minimal electronica, 70–100 BPM | Most-aware viewers; a brand film. Risk: weak as a cold opener |
| 2 Warm-human | Faces, sincere | 1.5–3 s | Humanist sans | Inspiring piano, uplifting pop | Founder / team-led |
| 3 Playful | Light humour, stickers | Cuts on punchlines | Rounded | Bouncy, quirky. Risk: the quirky bed reads as an explainer cliché | Skit, POV, listicle |
| 4 Energetic / kinetic | Beat-cut, slams, flash cuts | ~1 cut per beat or bar | Heavy condensed, 800–900 | Upbeat pop, future bass, 120–145 BPM | Cold problem → solution |
| 5 Chaotic / meme | Unhinged, trend audio, jump cuts | Sub-beat | Native caption fonts | Phonk, jersey club | Trend hijacks. Risk: youth-coded; test before a business audience |

- Pick the energy for the story and the hook. The app's category or risk class doesn't fix it.
- Low literacy means few big words, not slow cuts.
- The first 5 s show ≥ 3 visual states, at every level.
- Every film carries 4–5 kinds of attention trigger: an audio hit, a scene change, text, motion, a brand cue.
- Voice by energy: [directing-voice.md](./directing-voice.md) → **Voice presets per style**. Voice gaps by energy: [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Coverage and gaps**.

### Presets

Each preset is one combination of the dials around one signature device; every other layer stays plain. Take it whole or swap any dial. The Pixabay terms feed [directing-sound.md](./directing-sound.md) → **Pixabay searches by style**. The last three columns place the sold ≠ shown beats ([writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Sold vs shown**); in every music-led preset the drop and the device's first bar also stay voice-free ([writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **The spine and timelines by runtime**).

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Kinetic Slam** | Pain words slamming on the beat | Problem → solution · kinetic call-out · 4 | Paper world → flat brand planes · slam, scatter, mask punch-up · live UI in the phone | Shutter flash + match cut, zoom-through, dip, colour flood · pull-back, shake | `upbeat corporate`, `future bass upbeat` · clicks, ticks, ping, stamp | A plate or a line before the flash cut | After the flash cut | The slams, the flash cut | A pain kept on paper |
| 2 | **Exploded UI** | App pieces lifting out of the phone | As 1 | As 1; UI pieces lift out big, results fly out of the phone | As 1, + carry-across | As 1 | As 1 | After the flash cut | As 1 | 3–4 features in one flow |
| 3 | **Chat Chaos** | A generic chat flooding inside a drawn phone, then sorted | Overheard chat · pain shown · 4 | Phone UI · typewriter + slams · chat flood → order board | Morph (bubble → card), plain cuts · zoom-on-action | `tiktok upbeat`, `jersey club` · soft pops | A plate on the drop, voice silent | After the device's first bar | The overflow, the sort into the board | Orders or requests that arrive by chat |
| 4 | **Satisfying UI** | Macro close-ups snapping into place | Satisfying UI · visual oddity · 3 | Full-bleed UI · minimal type · cells and cards snapping | Hard cuts on the beat · punch-ins | `minimal technology`, `lofi chill` · dense clicks and ticks | A plate on the drop | Over the hook's first snaps, before the fill | The fill, the macros | Grid-heavy apps (schedules, timesheets) |
| 5 | **Before / After** | The split screen | Before / after · contrast · 3–4 | Paper steps vs one tap, never clocks · grotesk labels · comparison split | Sweeping wipe · static | `upbeat corporate` · one stamp on "after" | A voice line on the first "after" beat, its caption slot emptied | The first "after" app beat, over an emptied caption slot | The wipe, the callback rise, the seam sweep | Fewer-steps stories |
| 6 | **Data Hero** | Giant numbers as the visual | Result-first · number · 4 | Flat planes · numbers as type · data-as-type + brief phone proof; sample numbers keep the Sample chip | Roll → cut · punch-in | `future bass upbeat` · a pop on each number | A pre-drop voice line over the punch-ins | The first app frame, over a matching caption | The rolls, the wheels | Totals and counts the viewer already keeps |

| 9 | **Notes-app Lo-fi** | Phone-native type: a notes app or a text-to-speech read | Lo-fi native · pain as a list · 3 | Native UI look, no device frame, set in licensed font files · app proof at the end | Plain cuts | `lofi chill`, or a TTS voice · ticks | A note line by ~6–7 s | A note line, ≤ ~9.5 s | The app proof | Ad-resistant viewers |

Risk: weak as a cold opener |
| 11 | **Offer Blast** | The offer, slammed | Offer-first · offer first · 4 | Flat planes · slams · 2–3 app flashes | Hard cuts · shake | `upbeat pop` · pops | Frame 0 | ≤ 3 s | The slam | Retargeting: people who already engaged |
| 12 | **Teach-first** | The formula, worked out on screen | Edutainment · question · 2–3 | Swiss grid · masked rises · data-as-type for the worked figures, fact-checked | Morph · none | `minimal technology` · ticks | Spoken in the intro, by ~6–7 s | Spoken in the intro, ≤ ~9.5 s; the app returns at the end | The worked figure's landing | Ad-resistant viewers; a rule computed by hand |

- Founder Lo-fi is voice-led, so the voice is its clock. Notes-app Lo-fi and Teach-first are voice-clocked when voiced in full. Every other preset is music-led, with the music as the clock.

### Recommending

1. **Read the study:** the audience's awareness and objections, the old way and its artifact, the features to show, the proof ([studying-product.md](./studying-product.md) → **Study the audience**).
2. **Write 3 concepts for the promotion's audience that differ in message or blocker.** Three looks of one message are one concept.

   | Concept | Answers | Lead line comes from |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Relief | "Will this stop the pain?" | The pain in their words → the app doing that job |
   | Outcome | "What do I get?" | The result the screen shows |
   | Offer | "What's the catch?" | The Brief's offer, and the risk it removes |
   | Trust | "Who are these people?" | Who builds it, and how the work goes |

   Relief and outcome wording follows [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Core line, anchor phrase, value frame**.
3. **Dress each concept with the preset that fits the study:** its Fits column against the old way, the features and the awareness. Each card names its audience and one task (input → result), the message, the preset and its device, a one-line reason, and an energy level.
4. **The preset dresses the beats and never decides them.** The message plan and the approved beat sheet decide which beats exist and their order; the music decides when they land; the preset decides how they look. A preset that can't carry a required beat changes a dial, or gives way to another preset; the beat stays.
5. **A number is proof only after the viewer has seen its source.** A total or a count used as proof lands after the screen that produces it. A hook opens on a number only when the number carries its own meaning: counts of the viewer's own work ("12 workers. 6 days."), or a result the screen states in words ("12 payslips sent").
6. **The user picks a card, mixes dials across cards, or names their own.** The pick goes in [promotion-contract.md](./promotion-contract.md) → **promotion.md** → Spec: concept, preset, device, energy, clock.

Three cards for a bakery order app whose orders arrive by chat and land in a paper notebook:

| Concept | Message | Preset · device | Why it fits | Energy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relief | "Orders out of the chat, onto one board." | Chat Chaos · the chat flood sorting into the board | Orders arrive by chat today | 4 |
| Outcome | "Tomorrow's bake list, from today's orders." | Paper to App · the order notebook morphing into the bake list | The old way is a paper notebook | 3 |
| Trust | "We build it, and we answer your message." | Founder Lo-fi · a team member to camera | A new seller with no reviews to show | 2–3 |
