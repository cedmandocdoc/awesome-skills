### The jobs on a still

A still has no voice, so each job the voice carries in a video lands in read words or the picture. The **Message plan** names the zone or frame that owns each one.

| Job | On a single | On a carousel |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Who | The headline or the picture, at first sight | Frame 1 |
| 2 Pain and trigger | The headline or the old-way artifact | Frame 1 or 2 |
| 3 What it does for you | The picture proves it; the headline or subline states the consequence | The proof frames |
| 4 What is sold | The sold line in the B zone, and the disclosure line | The reveal frame |
| 5 Offer | The O zone: ≤ 6 words, naming exactly what is free | The last frame |
| 6–7 Next step and CTA | The CTA strip: the button's verb and the channel ("Message us on <channel>") | The last frame |
| T Trust beat | The who line, the brand and the disclosure line | The reveal and last frames |

- Jobs 2 and 3 are required in every still.
- The anchor phrase shows once in large type on a single (in a Statement, whose one line is the headline, the subline may carry it), and at least twice on a carousel (frame 1 and the last frame).
- Claims: a single holds ≤ 3 (the pain, the outcome, the offer); a carousel adds ≤ 1 per proof frame, up to 5. One fewer for SMB, mass-market and second-language audiences, never below the 3 a single holds.

### Word budgets

Read words are every word the viewer needs: headline, subline, labels, the offer, the CTA, the disclosure. The app's own UI text inside a screen isn't counted, but must be true. Chrome isn't counted either: the mark or its wordmark, the frame counter, step numerals; an eyebrow label is.

| Text | Words | Lines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Headline | ≤ 7 | ≤ 3 |
| Subline | ≤ 14 | ≤ 2 |
| Label, callout | ≤ 4 | 1 |
| CTA strip | ≤ 6: the verb and the channel | 1 |
| A whole single | ≤ 25 | — |
| A carousel frame | ≤ 20 | — |

- Older, second-language and low-literacy audiences get fewer words and the upper sizes.
- At most 3 sizes of read words in a frame: headline > subline > label.

### Headlines

The headline carries the promise: who it's for and why to look. It never carries the offer, except in Offer card.

| Formula | Example | Fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The pain, in their words | "Payday again, and the time book's a mess?" | Problem-aware; Pain call-out |
| Call-out by trade | "For every bakery owner:" | Niche trades |
| The result, as the screen states it | "12 payslips, sent." | Unaware; Result shown |
| Contrast pair | "The notebook, or one board." | Before / After |
| Count | "3 jobs your shop still does by hand" | Listicle |
| Question | "What if every order landed on one board?" | Cold |
| The sold line, plain | "Software that fits how you run." | Statement, Range |

- A still is muted, so its hook is the picture ([writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **From blocks to beats**): a question headline only beside the picture that hooks (the old-way artifact or the screen), never over a type-only ground.
- Write in the Brief's language and address. A line not in English is back-translated for the honesty scan and waits for the native check before any build.
- A number in a headline follows [choosing-style.md](./choosing-style.md) → **Concept cards** rule 5: counts of the viewer's own work, or a result the screen states in words.

### The frame sheet

One row per frame in `promotion.md` → **Frame sheet**:

| Column | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| Frame | `01`, `02`, … |
| Block | The structure's zone or frame it is |
| Job | Its jobs from **The jobs on a still** |
| Read words | Every read word, by role: H headline · S subline · L labels · CTA · D disclosure |
| Picture that proves it | Which source screen, state or artifact, and how it is shown (full-bleed, in a phone, a crop of one row, split) |
| Focal point | The one thing the eye lands on |
| Brand touch | The mark's position; the who line or the disclosure line when the frame holds it |

### The words-only read

Read every frame's read words alone, in order, with no picture. They must carry, in the structure's order: who it's for → the pain or the result → what is sold and who sells it → the offer → what to do. The disclosure line alone, at its small size, doesn't carry the builder: a subline or the last frame must. Checked at S1 and S2 ([checking-still.md](./checking-still.md)).
