# Choosing Style

## Overview

The film's form: the 15 dials and how to combine them, the hook types, the energy scale, the 13 presets, and how to dress the suggested structures as 3 concept cards. The script structure lives in [choosing-structure.md](./choosing-structure.md); what the film says in [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md); how each dial's options look, sound and are voiced in [directing-picture.md](./directing-picture.md), [directing-sound.md](./directing-sound.md) and [directing-voice.md](./directing-voice.md).

## Guidelines

### The dials

A promotion is a stack of independent dials. Choose each on its own; no dial sets the others.

**Combining them:**

- **One signature device per film:** the one thing a viewer remembers (the slams, the exploded UI, the collage). Name it in [promotion-contract.md](./promotion-contract.md) → **promotion.md** → Spec.
- **Every other layer in one grammar:** a wild device on a plain world, or a wild world with plain cuts.
- Transitions vary in kind, never in style ([directing-picture.md](./directing-picture.md) → **Transitions**).
- The brand kit rides unchanged over every device ([directing-picture.md](./directing-picture.md) → **Brand kit**).
- The clock: [directing-sound.md](./directing-sound.md) → **Music is the clock**; the voice-led presets are named under **Presets**.

### Hook types

| Hook | Frame 0 | Example | Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pain shown | The old way mid-task | The notebook mid-calculation; a generic chat with 47 unread inside a drawn phone | Problem-aware |
| Kinetic call-out | The pain in 2–4 words, slammed on the beat | "PAYDAY. AGAIN. BY HAND?" | Cold, music-led |
| Directive / warning | A command in large type | "Stop computing payroll by hand." | Problem-aware |
| Call-out by trade | The trade's own label | "To every bakery owner…" | Niche trades |
| If-then | A situation the viewer can name | "If your order book is still a chat thread…" | A nameable situation |
| Number | Counts from the viewer's own work | "12 workers. 6 days. 1 photo." | Sceptical viewers |
| Contrast | Two states of one task (paper / app) | The paper sheet beside the filled screen | Ready to switch |
| Question / curiosity gap | The real input, with a question | "What if one photo finished payroll?" | Cold |
| Visual oddity / surprise | Something impossible in frame 1 | The notebook page morphs into a phone screen | Cold |
| Result shown | The finished result as the screen shows it | "12 payslips sent" | Unaware |
| Offer first | The offer with what it covers | "Your first month, free" | Warm audiences |
| Deadline | A real date | "Year-end payroll closes on <date>. Ready?" | Seasonal, a real due date |
| Person speaks first | A team member's face and first line | "I build apps for clinics. Here's one." | Founder / team-led |
| Comment reply | A real comment from an earlier post | "'Can it do overtime?' Here's how." | After posts draw real comments |

The hook carries the structure's promise ([choosing-structure.md](./choosing-structure.md) → **The foundation**); its timing, word count and first line follow [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Hook lines**.

### Energy scale

| Level | Feel | Cut rate (start here; tune by eye) | Type | Music | Fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 Calm-premium | Spacious, one message | 2–4 s holds | Refined, 400–600 | Ambient, minimal electronica, 70–100 BPM | Most-aware viewers; a brand film. Risk: weak as a cold opener |
| 2 Warm-human | Faces, sincere | 1.5–3 s | Humanist sans | Inspiring piano, uplifting pop | Founder / team-led |
| 3 Playful | Light humour, stickers | Cuts on punchlines | Rounded | Bouncy, quirky. Risk: the quirky bed reads as an explainer cliché | Day-in-the-life, Listicle |
| 4 Energetic / kinetic | Beat-cut, slams, flash cuts | ~1 cut per beat or bar | Heavy condensed, 800–900 | Upbeat pop, future bass, 120–145 BPM | Cold Problem–Solution, PAS |
| 5 Chaotic / meme | Unhinged, trend audio, jump cuts | Sub-beat | Native caption fonts | Phonk, jersey club | Trend formats. Risk: youth-coded; test before a business audience |

- Pick the energy for the structure and the hook. The app's category or risk class doesn't fix it.
- This is the film's level; each beat's level around it is the curve ([choosing-structure.md](./choosing-structure.md) → **Energy curve**).
- Low literacy means few big words, not slow cuts.
- The first 5 s show ≥ 3 visual states, at every level.
- Every film carries 4–5 kinds of attention trigger: an audio hit, a scene change, text, motion, a brand cue.
- Voice by energy: [directing-voice.md](./directing-voice.md) → **Voice presets per style**. Voice gaps by energy: [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Coverage and gaps**.

### Presets

Each preset is one combination of the dials around one signature device; every other layer stays plain. Take it whole or swap any dial. The Pixabay terms feed [directing-sound.md](./directing-sound.md) → **Pixabay searches by style**. The structure places the promise, the sold line, the offer and the ask ([choosing-structure.md](./choosing-structure.md) → **The catalog**); the preset dresses them. In every music-led preset the drop and the device's first bar also stay voice-free ([writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Coverage and gaps**).

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Kinetic Slam** | Pain words slamming on the beat | Problem–Solution, PAS · kinetic call-out · 4 | Paper world → flat brand planes · slam, scatter, mask punch-up · live UI in the phone | Shutter flash + match cut, zoom-through, dip, colour flood · pull-back, shake | `upbeat corporate`, `future bass upbeat` · clicks, ticks, ping, stamp | The slams, the flash cut | A pain kept on paper |
| 2 | **Exploded UI** | App pieces lifting out of the phone | Feature–Benefit, Problem–Solution · kinetic call-out · 4 | As 1; UI pieces lift out big, results fly out of the phone | As 1, + carry-across | As 1 | Each lift | 3–4 features in one flow |
| 3 | **Chat Chaos** | A generic chat flooding inside a drawn phone, then sorted | Before–After–Bridge, Problem–Solution · pain shown · 4 | Phone UI · typewriter + slams · chat flood → order board | Morph (bubble → card), plain cuts · zoom-on-action | `tiktok upbeat`, `jersey club` · soft pops | The overflow, the sort into the board | Orders or requests that arrive by chat |
| 4 | **Satisfying UI** | Macro close-ups snapping into place | Quick demo · visual oddity · 3 | Full-bleed UI · minimal type · cells and cards snapping | Hard cuts on the beat · punch-ins | `minimal technology`, `lofi chill` · dense clicks and ticks | The fill, the macros | Grid-heavy apps (schedules, timesheets) |

Risk: weak as a cold opener |

- Founder Lo-fi is voice-led, so the voice is its clock. Notes-app Lo-fi and Teach-first are voice-clocked when narrated. Every other preset is music-led, with the music as the clock.
- A narrated film on a music-led preset places its lines between the device's moments; the voice-free floor still holds.
- Voice mode, read and duck per preset: [directing-voice.md](./directing-voice.md) → **Voice presets per style**.

### Concept cards

1. **Start from the three structures** suggested per [choosing-structure.md](./choosing-structure.md) → **Choosing**: one card each, so the cards differ in structure. Two cards on one structure differ in message or blocker; three looks of one message are one concept.
2. **Give each card its message angle**, from the study's audience:

   | Angle | Answers | Lead line comes from |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Relief | "Will this stop the pain?" | The pain in their words → the app doing that job |
   | Outcome | "What do I get?" | The result the screen shows |
   | Offer | "What's the catch?" | The Brief's offer, and the risk it removes |
   | Trust | "Who are these people?" | Who builds it, and how the work goes |

   Relief and outcome wording follows [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Core line, anchor phrase, value frame**.
3. **Dress each card with the preset that fits:** its Structures and Fits columns against the old way, the features and the awareness. Each card names its audience and one task (input → result), the structure, the length, the voice mode, the message, the preset and its device, a one-line reason, the main risk, and an energy level.
4. **The preset dresses the blocks and never decides them.** The structure and the approved beat sheet decide which beats exist and their order; the music decides when they land; the preset decides how they look. A preset that can't carry a required block changes a dial, or gives way to another preset; the block stays.
5. **A number is proof only after the viewer has seen its source.** A total or a count used as proof lands after the screen that produces it. A hook opens on a number only when the number carries its own meaning: counts of the viewer's own work ("12 workers. 6 days."), or a result the screen states in words ("12 payslips sent").
6. **The user picks a card, mixes dials across cards, or names their own.** The pick goes in [promotion-contract.md](./promotion-contract.md) → **promotion.md** → Spec: structure, length, concept, preset, device, energy, clock, voice mode.

Three cards for a bakery order app whose orders arrive by chat and land in a paper notebook, for cold owners, 30 s:

| Structure | Angle and message | Preset · device | Voice | Why it fits | Energy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Before–After–Bridge | Relief: "Orders out of the chat, onto one board." | Chat Chaos · the chat flood sorting into the board | Key lines | Orders arrive by chat today | 4 |
| Quick demo | Outcome: "Tomorrow's bake list, from today's orders." | Paper to App · the order notebook morphing into the bake list | None, or key lines | The old way is a paper notebook | 3 |
| Founder story | Trust: "We build it, and we answer your message." | Founder Lo-fi · a team member to camera | Speech on camera | A new seller with no reviews to show | 2–3 |
