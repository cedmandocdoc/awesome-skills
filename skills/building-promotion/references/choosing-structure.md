# Choosing Structure

## Overview

The script's shape: the foundation every structure keeps, the catalog of script structures with their blocks, lengths and voice modes, how to suggest three from the study, how a length sizes the blocks, the energy curve, and how what is sold frames any structure. The lines inside each block follow [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md); how the film looks follows [choosing-style.md](./choosing-style.md).

## Guidelines

### The foundation

Every structure keeps these; sources are under **References**.

| # | Rule | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The promise by ~3 s, the hook by ~6 s | By ~3 s the opening tells the viewer this is for them and why to keep watching. Each structure marks the block that carries it (**P**) and its form: the problem, the result, the count, the contrast, the founder's why. Only Teaser withholds the answer, and it still gives a reason to stay by 3 s |
| 2 | One message | One audience, one task, one core line ([writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Core line, anchor phrase, value frame**) |
| 3 | Show it working | The picture carries the story; each claim is proven on screen while it is made |
| 4 | Brand early, in short touches | Per [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Brand touches**: seen by ~3–5 s, pulsed through the film, never a logo card that blocks the hook |
| 5 | Open fast, then peaks | At least 2 peaks after the hook (**Energy curve**); never a slow build to a late reveal |
| 6 | Readable muted, better with sound | Every fact the viewer needs to act is on screen; the voice adds meaning, and captions carry its key words ([writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Voice, captions, picture**) |
| 7 | The voice says what the picture can't | The meaning of a beat, the seller and the offer; how much is the voice mode ([directing-voice.md](./directing-voice.md) → **Coverage**) |
| 8 | People speaking beat a voice-over | A real person on camera is the strongest voice. A film made from UI alone gives that up; say so when it matters |
| 9 | The ask after the context | The offer and the CTA come once the viewer knows the problem and what the app does, except in structures for viewers who already want it (Teaser, Offer-led). The ask holds ≥ 2 s |
| 10 | True and proven | [promotion-contract.md](./promotion-contract.md) → **Honesty rules** |
| 11 | The structure isn't the main lever | Concepts differ in structure and message; the craft inside each block decides more |

### The catalog

Shares of runtime and lengths are starting values; tune them by eye. Block marks: **P** carries the promise; **B** names the brand, with the sold line when sold ≠ shown; **O** carries the offer and the CTA. Voice modes per [directing-voice.md](./directing-voice.md) → **Coverage**, the default first.

| Structure | Family | Blocks, % of runtime | Min · natural · max s | Below the min | From the source alone | Voice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem–Solution | Problem-led | Problem P 15 · Reveal B 12 · Proof 48 · Payoff 12 · Ask O 13 | 12 · 15–30 · 60 | Quick demo | Yes, when the old way has a showable artifact | Key lines, narrated, none | Study + platform |
| PAS | Problem-led | Problem P 12 · Agitate 20 · Solve B 10 · Proof 45 · Ask O 13 | 18 · 30–60 · 90 | Problem–Solution | Yes; the agitation is thinner without people | Narrated, key lines | Copy canon |
| Before–After–Bridge | Problem-led | Before P 25 · After 22 · Bridge B 38 · Ask O 15 | 10 · 15–30 · 60 | One split, before → after | Yes | Key lines, none, narrated | Folklore |
| Comparison (old way vs new) | Show-led | Contrast P 10 · Splits B 70 · Verdict + ask O 20 | 6 · 15–30 · 60 | One split | Yes | Key lines, none, narrated | Meta-analysis |
| Feature–Benefit | Show-led | Promise P+B 12 · Pairs 70 · Restate 8 · Ask O 10 | 6 · 15–30 · 60 | One pair | Yes | Key lines, narrated, none | Study + canon |
| Listicle | Show-led | Count hook P+B 10 · Items 75 · Bonus + ask O 15 | 12 · 20–34 · 60 | — | Yes | Key lines, narrated, none | Platform |
| Quick demo | Show-led | Result first P+B 12 · Action 60 · Payoff 10 · Ask O 18 | 5 · 6–15 · 30 | — | Yes, ideal | None, key lines | Platform + study |
| How-to | Show-led | "How to" hook P 10 · Steps B 65 · Result 10 · Ask O 15 | 20 · 30–60 · 180 | Quick demo, one tip | Yes | Narrated | Platform |
| Explainer | Show-led | Problem P 10 · Old ways fail 12 · Solution B 8 · How it works 40 · Benefits 15 · Ask O 15 | 30 · 60–90 · 180 | Problem–Solution | Yes | Narrated | Canon |
| Day-in-the-life | Moment-led | Opening moment P 12 · Moments B 65 · Day's-end payoff 10 · Ask O 13 | 15 · 30–60 · 90 | Three moments | Partly: timed UI moments, weaker without people | Key lines, none | Study + canon |
| Founder story | People-led | Why P+B 12 · Problem seen 22 · What was built 33 · Promise 15 · Ask O 18 | 30 · 45–90 · 120 | A reminder cut of a longer film | Partly: the founder's own voice over the UI | Narrated, by the founder | Thin study |
| Testimonial | People-led | Result P 12 · Before 18 · What changed B 40 · Recommendation 10 · Ask O 20 | 15 · 30–60 · 90 | A consented quote card | No: a consenting customer | Speech on camera | Study |
| Creator | People-led | Creator + claim P 10 · Context 18 · Try B 45 · Verdict 10 · Ask O 17 | 15 · 15–30 · 60 | — | No: a real creator | Speech on camera | Platform |
| Teaser | Moment-led | Tease P 50 · Reveal B 25 · Date + ask O 25 | 5 · 6–15 · 20 | — | Yes, with a real date or version | None, key lines | Platform, thin |
| Offer-led | Moment-led | Offer P+B+O 25 · What you get 40 · How to claim + ask O 35 | 5 · 6–15 · 20 | — | Yes | None, key lines | Canon + platform |

- **Evidence:** "Study" means tested effects on recall, persuasion or sales; "Platform" means a platform's own guidance; "Canon" and "Folklore" mean practitioner formats with little or no testing.
- An entry not listed (a skit, a trend format) is built as the nearest entry: a skit is a labelled dramatization inside Day-in-the-life; a trend rides a Quick demo or a Listicle.

### Scaling and cut order

A block grows by repeating its unit; a short film cuts in the order given, then switches to the short form below the minimum rather than shaving every block evenly.

| Structure | Grows by | Cut first, in order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem–Solution | A second proof at 45–60 s, then a trust beat | The second proof → the payoff folds into the ask → a shorter reveal |
| PAS | Consequences: 1 at 30 s, 2–3 at 60–90 s | Consequences to one → proof depth → the problem merges into the hook |
| Before–After–Bridge | Bridge steps | Before detail → bridge steps |
| Comparison | Splits: 1 at 6 s, 2 at 15 s, 3–4 at 30 s | Down to the one decisive split → the verdict merges into the ask |
| Feature–Benefit | Pairs: 1 at 6 s, 2 at 15 s, 3 at 30 s, 4–5 at 60 s | The weakest pair → the restate |
| Listicle | Items, ≤ 4 s each: 3 at 15 s, 3–5 at 30 s, 5–7 at 60 s | Middle items → the bonus; keep the strongest first and a strong last |
| Quick demo | — (past ~30 s it becomes a How-to) | Intermediate steps → the payoff text |
| How-to | Steps: 1 tip at 15 s, 3–5 at 45–60 s | Merge steps; the result stays |
| Explainer | Mechanism steps: 2–3 | The benefit list → "old ways fail" folds into the hook → the mechanism to 2 steps |
| Day-in-the-life | Moments: 3 at 15–30 s, 4–6 at 60 s | Moments, keeping the pain moment and the relief moment → the day's-end payoff |
| Founder story | Origin detail | Origin detail → the promise line; keep "why I built it" in one sentence |
| Testimonial | What changed, in the speaker's words | The backstory → the recommendation |
| Creator, Teaser, Offer-led | — | The context, the tease or the "what you get" detail |

Never cut, at any length: the promise, one proof, the brand cue and the ask; when sold ≠ shown, the sold line.

### Choosing

Suggest; the user decides. What is sold never picks the structure (**Framing: what is sold**).

1. **Read the inputs** from `product.md`:

   | Input | From | Read as |
   | --- | --- | --- |
   | Awareness | Study → the audience | Else: a novel category → problem-aware; a known category and a cold audience → solution-aware; retargeting → product- or most aware |
   | Objective | Brief → Objective | Else from the CTA: a message, a sign-up, an install → action; a follow or a watch → awareness |
   | Involvement | Study → risk class and the features to show | High: ≥ 4 steps to the first result, a new mechanism, or money, health, legal or records at stake; else low |
   | Old way | Study → the old way and its artifact | A showable artifact favours Before–After–Bridge, Comparison and Problem–Solution |
   | People | Study → Proof and the structure inputs | A consenting customer, the founder willing to speak, a creator |
   | Placement and length | Brief → Placement, Length | Sound-on or muted-first; a hard length or a suggestion |

2. **Filter.** Drop structures that need a person the promotion lacks: Testimonial without a consenting customer's quote in **Proof**, Creator without a creator, Founder story without the founder's own voice (never an AI voice presented as the founder). Day-in-the-life never shows a stock, illustrated or AI person using the app: anonymous hands only. Under a hard length, drop structures whose minimum is longer, unless their short form fits; offer the short form and say so.
3. **Score** each survivor 0–2 on each row below: 2 when it is listed for the input, 1 when an adjacent value lists it, else 0.

   | Awareness | Fits best |
   | --- | --- |
   | Unaware | Day-in-the-life, Before–After–Bridge, Founder story, PAS opened from the situation |
   | Problem-aware | PAS, Problem–Solution, Before–After–Bridge, How-to |
   | Solution-aware | Quick demo, Feature–Benefit, Comparison, Listicle, Explainer (a new mechanism) |
   | Product-aware | Testimonial, Comparison, Listicle, a Quick demo of one feature |
   | Most aware | Offer-led, Teaser, a short Quick demo |

   | Objective | Fits best |
   | --- | --- |
   | Awareness | Day-in-the-life, Founder story, Before–After–Bridge, Teaser, a short Quick demo |
   | Consideration | Quick demo, Explainer, How-to, Feature–Benefit, Comparison, Testimonial |
   | Action | Problem–Solution, PAS, Listicle, Testimonial; Offer-led for warm audiences only |

   | Involvement | Fits best |
   | --- | --- |
   | Low | Problem–Solution, Quick demo, Before–After–Bridge, Listicle; short lengths fine |
   | High | PAS, Explainer, How-to, Testimonial; 30–90 s, narrated |

4. **Suggest three**, the top scorer of each family: problem-led, show-led, people- or moment-led. A family with no survivor gives its place to the next best of another family, and the card says why. Each suggestion states the inputs it matched, its length (**Length**), its default voice mode, and its main risk. The user confirms one, swaps one, or names their own; their choice wins.

### Length

| The Brief's length | Do |
| --- | --- |
| Hard (the user's, or the placement's cap) | A guard: the runtime is the length, ± one beat of the track, never over a cap. Reserve the fixed minimums first: the promise by 3 s (the hook ≥ 1.5 s), the brand cue by 5 s, the ask held ≥ 2 s, each proof long enough to read ([writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Reading budget per window**). Split the rest by the blocks' shares, then snap each block to the grid (whole bars in a music-led film). Below the structure's minimum, use its short form |
| Suggested, or none | The structure's natural range, inside the placement's norm from the Brief; the long end for high involvement, the short end for low. For several cuts, offer a ladder (60 → 30 → 15 → 6 s); a ≤ 6 s cut is its own idea (Quick demo, Teaser, Offer-led), never a trim |

- The music sets the final seconds inside the guard ([directing-sound.md](./directing-sound.md) → **Cutting**).
- A ≤ 15 s cut of a longer film uses its structure's short form.

### Energy curve

Each beat carries an energy level 1–5 in the beat sheet; the film's overall level stays a style dial ([choosing-style.md](./choosing-style.md) → **Energy scale**), and the curve moves around it.

| Block | Starting level |
| --- | --- |
| The hook | 4 |
| Problem, agitate, before | 2–3: the dip where the pain lands |
| The reveal, the first result | 5: the first peak |
| Proof, steps, items, splits | 3–4, rising |
| The payoff, the after, the verdict | 5: the second peak |
| The ask | 3: a warm settle |
| The end card | 2 |

- Teaser climbs to the reveal; Offer-led stays high; a Quick demo is one sustained climb.
- The curve picks the music's sections ([directing-sound.md](./directing-sound.md) → **Cutting**) and the voice's reads ([directing-voice.md](./directing-voice.md) → **Read, emotion arc, emphasis**).

### Framing: what is sold

What is sold rides on any structure, per [writing-script.md](./writing-script.md) → **Sold vs shown**: the sold line goes in the block marked **B**, the offer and the CTA in the block marked **O**, and the disclosure at the app's first appearance ([promotion-contract.md](./promotion-contract.md) → **Disclosure**). An offer that lands before the viewer knows what is built says whose it is.
